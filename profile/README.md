# OneThreeFive

We're **135 Main Community Church** in Springdale, Newfoundland: a small church with contemporary, Spirit-led worship and a big welcome.

We build our own software to help the church care for every person: an app and website that make sure nobody is missed, and that people get the information, connections and support they need. Some of it we share openly, for other churches to use.

**Serve God with your craft.** If you write code and are looking for a church home where your skills matter, we'd love to meet you: [join our Technology Team](https://135main.org/teams/).
